# Katherine Isbister - How Games Move Us

Source PDF: `../Game Design Reading and Principles/Isbister How Games Move Us.pdf`

## Synoptic Note

Isbister is useful because the game risks becoming intellectually correct but
emotionally flat. Games move players through agency, choice, embodiment, social
framing, and intimacy with systems or characters. Tokenizer Training should make
players feel tension, embarrassment, competence, irritation, recovery, and dark
amusement. The emotional design should come from action and consequence, not
from long narrative copy.

## On Thinking Playfully

The opening frames playful thinking as a way to explore difficult ideas without
reducing them to lectures.

Implementation consequence: the tutorial should let players try, fail, and
recover rather than explain every tokenization rule in advance.

## Introduction

The introduction positions games as emotionally distinctive because they combine
agency with designed feedback.

Implementation consequence: the player must own the cuts. Review feedback should
be sharp enough to support responsibility without feeling arbitrary.

## Chapter 1 - A Series of Interesting Choices

Choices become interesting when they are consequential, interpretable, and tied
to a player's evolving understanding.

Implementation consequence: each cut should be a small bet. The player should
understand why adding a cut can help or hurt before the session is over.

## Chapter 2 - Social Play

Games create emotion through social roles, judgment, cooperation, competition,
and comparison, even when the other participant is fictional or systemic.

Implementation consequence: the overseer voice should behave like a social
pressure source: brief, technical, irritated, and sometimes funny, but never
random.

## Chapter 3 - Bodies at Play

Bodily input and physical performance shape emotion. Timing, movement, and touch
make abstract systems personal.

Implementation consequence: swiping across text is the emotional bridge between
computational abstraction and player skill. Mobile touch feel matters.

## Chapter 4 - Bridging Distance to Create Intimacy and Connection

Games can create closeness across distance by aligning action, feedback, and
attention.

Implementation consequence: the player should develop intimacy with tokenization
patterns through repeated near-misses, not through external lore.

## Endgame

The closing emphasis is that game emotion is designed through the full
interactive situation.

Implementation consequence: playtest readiness depends on how the tutorial,
timer, economy, cuts, feedback, and fiction feel together.
