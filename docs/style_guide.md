# Style Guide

## Visual Tone

The game should look like polished contemporary AI software whose purpose is degraded human tokenization labor.

Use:
- warm off-white panels, soft mint/teal surfaces, quiet amber action cues, muted coral warnings, and dark readable text
- spacious interface chrome with restrained top bars, audit labels, model-status pills, and understated compliance marks
- a segmentation lane with faint rails, timing ticks, a readable cut band, and subtle route-acquisition cues
- translucent background movement that supports the static prompt without competing with it
- polished product spacing and stable controls
- diegetic bureaucratic copy: manual route, audit window, biological fallback, help disabled, margin preservation
- a small pixel-art hot dog assistant named Wiener in the play scene and near-action speech

Avoid:
- neon cyberpunk
- hacker terminals
- literal factories, rollers, crates, machinery, or an industrial conveyor belt
- old operating-system beige
- static SaaS dashboard composition
- cute wellness mascot energy
- heroic sword/fantasy visuals
- UI copy that explains the premise as "a dated 2026 AI browser used in 2040"
- design-description prose inside the game

## Motion

- Swipe should feel responsive and tactile.
- Correct cuts should snap or flash.
- Missed cuts should appear clearly.
- False cuts should feel costly but not confusing.
- Feedback should linger long enough to read.
- The text is the moving object. Conveyor-belt feeling comes from lane motion grammar, not from drawing a factory belt.
- The active cut band should be readable without competing with the prompt.

## Sound Direction

Contemporary AI-interface sound:
- short sine or triangle tones
- soft brushed cut plus granular click impression
- muted pill-button taps
- gentle confirmation swell
- small ascending glassy chime for clean resolution
- soft negative notification for errors
- low modern attention pulse for warnings

Avoid square-wave arcade bleeps, Pong sounds, sirens, coin sounds, sword slashes, and loud retro effects.

## Overseer Voice

Wiener should be:
- dry
- technical
- bureaucratic
- faintly irritated
- short
- educational first, funny second
- petty without becoming crude
- aware of state: idle, active, correct, missed, false cut, overcut, low balance

Examples:
- Biological fallback active.
- Human segmentation route assigned.
- Help disabled to preserve margin.
- Accuracy before interpretation.
- The boundary survived contact with biology.
- The model noticed. It had budget for that.

Avoid:
- long monologues during active play
- generic encouragement
- fake semantic hallucinations
- sitcom punchlines
- explaining the fiction directly
