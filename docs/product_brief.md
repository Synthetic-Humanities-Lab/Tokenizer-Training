# Tokenization Training

Tokenization Training is a browser-first mobile arcade game about tokenization from WienerWorks' Human Segmentation Division.

The visible hierarchy is:

1. Tokenization Training as the product/training surface.
2. WienerWorks as the company mark.
3. Human Segmentation Division as the internal department.

WienerWorks references Norbert Wiener and cybernetics, but the assistant is deliberately a small pixel-art hot dog named Wiener. The joke should sit inside a polished interface, not on top of it.

The player is assigned degraded human segmentation labor inside clean contemporary AI-product software. Automated segmentation is expensive, untrusted, or unavailable, so the system trains humans to manually predict OpenAI-style token boundaries. Each round presents a text string. The player swipes across the string to cut predicted token boundaries. The system reveals the true tokenizer segmentation, scores accuracy, applies wage/cost consequences, and advances.

The design premise belongs in product and visual-direction docs. In-game copy should remain diegetic: audit windows, disabled help, manual routes, margin preservation, payroll, and biological fallback.

## Tone

Polished AI software whose purpose is degraded human labor. Clean, warm, spacious, interface-native, calm, slightly too polite, and bureaucratically hostile. Wiener is snarky and technically useful, but the teaching remains brief and concrete.

## Core Loop

1. Text appears.
2. Player swipes/cuts predicted token boundaries.
3. True tokenization resolves.
4. Correct cuts, missed cuts, false cuts, token count, and cost are shown.
5. Session continues until balance reaches zero.

## Primary Learning

- Words are not always tokens.
- Spaces matter.
- Punctuation and formatting matter.
- Weird strings can become expensive.
- Tokenization is a computational transformation, not normal reading.
