# Concept 07 - Playtest Gates

Draws on: Zubek prototyping and playtesting; Swink metrics; Tufte evidence;
Isbister agency; Flanagan critical play.

## Gate 1 - First Action

Pass condition: a new player can start Tutorial, understand that swiping creates
cuts between text units, and resolve a round without external explanation.

Evidence to collect: hesitation before first swipe, accidental button presses,
and whether the player uses the target hints.

## Gate 2 - Boundary Learning

Pass condition: after two review states, the player can explain at least one
non-word tokenization behavior such as leading spaces, punctuation splitting, or
dense-string fragmentation.

Evidence to collect: spoken explanation or post-play question, plus cut accuracy
on later examples.

## Gate 3 - Input Trust

Pass condition: missed or false cuts are attributed to player prediction, not
gesture ambiguity.

Evidence to collect: complaints about snap positions, finger occlusion, mouse
tracking, or marker timing.

## Gate 4 - Economy Literacy

Pass condition: the player can read pay, cost, net, and balance after a review
state and understands that false speculative cuts can hurt.

Evidence to collect: player explanation of one round's net result.

## Gate 5 - Critical Frame

Pass condition: the player recognizes the degraded AI labor satire without the
game stopping to explain it.

Evidence to collect: player comments about the browser, Wiener, ranks,
training framing, or cost-recovery premise.

## Gate 6 - Visual Readability

Pass condition: the game remains readable at desktop and portrait mobile
viewport sizes, with no overlap between text, controls, feedback, and Wiener
speech.

Evidence to collect: screenshots of menu, active round, review state, and
results at representative viewports.

## Gate 7 - Engagement And Aesthetic Intent

Pass condition: the player shows some pull toward continued play and reads the
degraded assistant-browser visual style as intentional rather than broken,
generic, or accidental.

Evidence to collect: voluntary next-round behavior, comments about wanting to
continue or stop, and quotes about the visual style, degraded browser frame,
Wiener, or interface feeling deliberate.
